# 상황 설명
슬라이드, 차트, 데이터 설명 시 사용

# 기본 표현
- "As you can see on this chart..."
- "The key takeaway from this slide is..."
- "Let me walk you through the numbers."
- "This graph shows the trend over the last six months."
- "The most important point here is..."

# 응용 문장
- "Comparing Q3 and Q4, we see a 15% increase in sales."
- "This graph illustrates customer growth by region."
- "Revenue from product A has increased steadily."
- "The main driver for this trend is our new campaign."

# Role-play 연습
| 내가 말하는 문장 | 상대방 예상 반응 | 내 대응 |
|----------------|----------------|---------|
| "As you can see, sales increased by 15%." | "What caused the increase?" | "The main factor was the marketing campaign." |
| "Key takeaway: customer retention is improving." | "Any insights on how?" | "Yes, loyalty programs and personalized support." |
| "This graph shows a 10% increase in Q4 revenue." | "Is that adjusted for seasonality?" | "Good point, yes, these figures are seasonally adjusted." |
