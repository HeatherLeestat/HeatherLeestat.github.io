# 상황 설명
회의 마무리, action item 확인, 감사 인사

# 기본 표현
- "To summarize today’s discussion..."
- "The action items are as follows..."
- "Thank you all for your time and input."
- "Let’s reconvene next week to review progress."
- "I’ll follow up via email with the meeting notes."

# 응용 문장
- "All tasks are assigned and deadlines confirmed."
- "Please review the summary and let me know if anything is missing."
- "Looking forward to our next meeting."

# Role-play 연습
| 내가 말하는 문장 | 상대방 예상 반응 | 내 대응 |
|----------------|----------------|---------|
| "Key action items are X, Y, Z." | "Got it." | "I’ll send the summary by EOD." |
| "Thank you all for your time." | "Thanks." | "See you next week." |
| "Please confirm the tasks are clear." | "Confirmed." | "Perfect, thanks for the prompt response." |
