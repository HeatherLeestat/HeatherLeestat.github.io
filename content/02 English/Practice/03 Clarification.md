# 상황 설명
상대방이 내 말을 잘못 이해했을 때

# 기본 표현
- "Just to clarify, what I meant was..."
- "I think there might be a misunderstanding. Let me explain..."
- "Actually, the point I was making is..."
- "Let me rephrase that to avoid confusion."
- "I’d like to clarify my previous point."

# 응용 문장
- "The report might have been interpreted differently. What I intended to say is..."
- "To make sure we’re on the same page regarding the timeline, the deadline is next Friday."
- "There seems to be some confusion about the data."
- "Let me clarify the key assumptions behind this figure."

# Role-play 연습
| 내가 말하는 문장 | 상대방 예상 반응 | 내 대응 |
|----------------|----------------|---------|
| "The deadline is next Friday." | "I thought it was this Friday." | "Yes, sorry for the confusion." |
| "Misunderstanding about report data." | "Can you explain?" | "The key point: sales figures refer to last quarter." |
| "Just to clarify, our target is Q4 revenue." | "Oh, I understood Q3." | "Exactly, Q4 is our focus." |
