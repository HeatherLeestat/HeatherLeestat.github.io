# 상황 설명
회의에서 의견이나 아이디어 제시

# 기본 표현
- "In my opinion, we should consider..."
- "I would suggest that we try..."
- "One idea might be to..."
- "It might be worth exploring..."
- "I recommend focusing on..."

# 응용 문장
- "Focusing on customer retention could bring better results."
- "Recommend allocating more budget to marketing."
- "We might benefit from streamlining the approval process."
- "Introducing a new tool could save time."

# Role-play 연습
| 내가 말하는 문장 | 상대방 예상 반응 | 내 대응 |
|----------------|----------------|---------|
| "I suggest increasing the marketing budget." | "Do you have data?" | "Yes, ROI improved by 20% last quarter." |
| "Streamline the approval process." | "That could work." | "Exactly, it would reduce delays." |
| "We might focus more on customer retention." | "Good idea." | "Great, we can plan specific initiatives." |
